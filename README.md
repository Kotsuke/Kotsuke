### About Me

I am an **Informatics Engineering student** with a strong passion for **Full Stack Development**, **Artificial Intelligence**, and **Software Engineering**. I enjoy building applications that solve real-world problems, ranging from web and mobile apps to AI-powered systems. 

- I'm currently focused on improving my skills in **Frontend & Backend Development**, **Databases**, and **Cloud Technologies**.

- Throughout my academic journey, I have worked on various projects involving **Flutter**, **Flask**, **Laravel**, **Next.js**, **MySQL**, **Oracle APEX**, and **Computer Vision (YOLOv8)**.
